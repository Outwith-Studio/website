Outwith is a studio working on human agency.

We work on products, tools, and ideas for people and organisations who insist on being the authors of their best work.

We’re interested in the fine line between what technology enables and what it degrades: optimising for how we can participate more deliberately in the world.

Our first product is _Truffle_—a platform that helps people to ask better questions.

Outwith is Monica Park, Calum Forsyth and Michael Heins.
