I About Outwith is a global studio for products, innovation and ideas. Born out of a desire to look beyond typical short-termism, it is a place for us to spend time with ideas that we want to see in the world. One half innovation consultancy, one half idea incubator, we work on select projects across technology, social impact, and innovation strategy to drive change at scale.
II Writing Each season we investigate a theme through research, writing and building.
